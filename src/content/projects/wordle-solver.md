## Overview

A companion for getting better at Wordle rather than a machine that solves it for you. Play any day's puzzle, and at every step it shows the words that are still possible. A toggle switches between valid next words (guesses that respect every clue so far) and all words, so you can see what a strong next guess looks like without being told the answer.

## The idea

The point is training intuition: seeing how each guess narrows the field, and why some guesses cut the list far more than others.

## What I took from it

A clean lesson in information and entropy, and in designing a tool that teaches rather than just answers.
