## Overview

Spotify will hand you years of raw listening history. The idea was to make that data tell a story: rolling streaming trends, personal rankings, song metadata, and how the emotional tone of what I listen to shifts over time. As a fun end point, it pulls my top tracks and clips them into a mashup.

## What I took from it

Personal data is a great motivator for learning data wrangling, and a lot of "insight" is just choosing a good rolling window.
