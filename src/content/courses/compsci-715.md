## Overview

A research-flavoured graphics course focused on current AR/VR and computer graphics problems. Rather than just lectures, I implemented a solution to a graphics research problem, evaluated it and wrote it up as a report and presentation.

## Topics covered

### Research-driven graphics

- Current research issues in computer graphics
- Reading and critiquing research papers
- Choosing and scoping a research problem

### Advanced rendering and VR

- Real-time rendering techniques
- AR/VR technologies and interaction
- Performance and visual quality trade-offs

### Research skills

- Implementing and analysing a graphics solution
- Designing evaluations of a technique
- Communicating results in reports and presentations

## Skills and tools

Computer graphics, real-time rendering, AR/VR, shader programming, research methods, technical writing, presentation, evaluation design

## Assessment

- Individual research project (implementation)
- Written research report
- Presentation
- Class participation and discussion
