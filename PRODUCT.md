# Product: John Moore — personal portfolio

## What it is
Static Astro site showcasing John Moore's projects, courses, experience and CV. Hosted free (Cloudflare Pages / GitHub Pages). Near-zero client JS.

## Who
John Moore — Master of Engineering (Bioengineering) candidate in the Empathic Computing Lab, University of Auckland (https://empathiccomputing.org/). Previously BE(Hons) First Class, Software Engineering. Graduate Teaching Assistant.

## Audience
- Technical recruiters: scan in 30 seconds for skills keywords, roles, stack, outcomes.
- PhD supervisors / researchers: want depth, process, research thinking.
- Often older readers: legible type sizes, high contrast, obvious navigation, no gimmicks that hide content.

## Surfaces & modes
- Home (single long scroll, sticky top nav, CV button): Experience mode, but scannable.
- Project pages: Read mode — design-journal / case study (spec sidebar + journal body with figures).
- Course pages: Read mode — John-authored course detail.

## Principles
- Content first; depth comes from layering and craft, not effects.
- Must not look AI-generated or templated. Engineer's field notebook, not SaaS landing page.
- Amber = action only. Sky = structure/primary. Navy = fog/ground.
- Everything reachable from the home page; scroll + page navigation only.
