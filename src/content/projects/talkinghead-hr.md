## Overview

A planned study where participants watch emotional VR playback of a high-fidelity talking avatar while shown a heart-rate cue. The cue is sometimes real and sometimes spoofed.

## Why

Two questions: can heart-rate feedback be faked convincingly, and how much does perceived heart rate affect trust and perception of the avatar?

## What I built

- Unity VR project with a high-fidelity head scene (`HDHead`) and a Character Creator-based avatar pipeline.
