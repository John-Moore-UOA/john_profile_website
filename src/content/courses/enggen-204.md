## Overview

A course on communicating and collaborating as an engineer. Most of the work was in teams, learning how high-functioning teams operate and how to fix them when they do not.

## Topics covered

### Teamwork

- Team formation and roles
- Recognising and correcting team dysfunction
- Conflict management
- Group decision-making

### Communication

- Modes of discourse
- Reflective practice
- Giving and receiving feedback

### Collaboration and professional context

- Participation and perspective-taking
- Social regulation in groups
- Health and safety, sustainability and ethics
- Considering stakeholders

## Skills and tools

Teamwork, communication, reflective practice, giving feedback, conflict resolution, stakeholder analysis, professional ethics

## Assessment

- Group coursework
- Quizzes and individual assessments
- Tutorial attendance and participation
