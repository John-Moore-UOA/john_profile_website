## Overview

This course is about how to represent a real-world problem inside a computer so it can be solved by search, logic and probabilistic reasoning. I worked through five modules covering search, logic, planning, uncertainty and natural language processing, and finished with a look at the ethics of AI in society.

## Topics covered

### Search and games

- Uninformed and informed search strategies
- Designing heuristics as rules of thumb for prioritising options
- Constraint satisfaction problems
- Adversarial search for game playing

### Logic and knowledge representation

- Propositional and predicate logic
- Logical inference and deduction
- Declarative rule-based systems, data-driven and goal-driven
- Logic programming in Prolog

### Planning

- Representing world states and actions
- Classical planning algorithms
- Predicting how decisions change the state of the world

### Reasoning under uncertainty

- Bayesian networks
- Markov chains
- Utility theory and decision networks

### Natural language and AI in society

- Fundamental NLP tools and representations
- Information retrieval, question answering and text mining
- History, future and ethics of AI

## Skills and tools

Heuristic search, A*, constraint satisfaction, Prolog, predicate logic, automated planning, Bayesian networks, Markov models, decision networks, NLP basics, AI ethics
