## Overview

A course on turning a problem into a validated, realistic innovation. In teams I explored a problem through the UN Sustainable Development Goals, ideated solutions and built a business case and implementation plan.

## Topics covered

### Creativity and innovation

- Creative problem-solving methods
- Ideation techniques
- Assessing and choosing between options

### Problem space exploration

- Understanding customers and society
- UN Sustainable Development Goals framework
- Writing a problem statement

### Business case development

- Value proposition and viability
- Costs, benefits and impact
- Presenting a case to stakeholders

### Project management fundamentals

- Planning and scheduling
- Risk and resources
- Implementation planning

## Skills and tools

Project management, innovation, ideation, business case development, stakeholder analysis, UN SDGs, teamwork, presentation

## Assessment

- Group innovation project
- Quizzes
- Individual assignment
- Tutorial participation
