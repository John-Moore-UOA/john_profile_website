## Overview

A team capstone. The idea was zero-configuration hardware: each Raspberry Pi broadcasts its presence on the local network, the backend discovers and registers it, and a React frontend lets people monitor and control devices without knowing IP addresses.

![Power monitoring dashboard](/images/projects/capstone-device-platform/2.webp "Live current, voltage and power for a team, checked against its class power limit")

![Capstone hardware and laptops on the lab bench](/images/projects/capstone-device-platform/1.webp "Testing on the lab bench")

## What I took from it

Working as a team over a long project, and how much of "IoT" is really discovery, networking and failure handling.
