## Overview

How should you type a PIN in VR when anyone nearby can watch your hands? This project compared head-ray and hand-ray interaction on a randomised PIN pad, looking at speed, errors, workload and how guessable the inputs were.

## My part

The Unity side (PIN pad randomisation, ray interaction) and the analysis pipeline: statistical notebooks per study, PIN-strength calculations and transcription of participants' audio memos.

## What I learned

Running a real user study end to end, and that the analysis plan should exist before the first participant arrives.
